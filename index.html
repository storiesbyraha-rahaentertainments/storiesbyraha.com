About Stories By RAHA

Hi, we're so glad you're here.

We believe a wedding is more than a beautiful celebration. It is a collection of fleeting moments — a nervous smile before the ceremony, a parent's quiet tear, friends laughing between rituals, hands held a little tighter, and the people you love coming together under one roof.

Stories By RAHA was born from a simple belief: the most meaningful photographs are the ones that bring you back to how it felt.

We approach every wedding with a balance of honest storytelling and thoughtful artistry. We don't want your photographs to simply show you what happened. We want them to make you feel it again — years from now, when the flowers are gone, the music has faded, and all that remains are the memories you chose to preserve.

From the grand celebrations to the moments nobody planned for, we look for the emotion, the connection and the little details that make your story uniquely yours.

Because every couple has a story. We simply make sure yours is never forgotten.
